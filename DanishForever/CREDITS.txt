Gossip data: Danish text translated by Claude. Lookup keys and hashing method based on PolishForever by Jan Slomian (code MIT, data CC BY 4.0), https://github.com/JSlomian/PolishForever

Quest text: about 330 quests that exist only in this client, and the progress/hand-in texts of about 450 older quests, were translated from the Polish text of PolishForever (data CC BY 4.0), because no English source was available.
English names and strings that were missing (NPCs, items, spells, Classic Era quests) were found by comparing with the English game-text tables of the MultiLanguage addon by Ruben Zantingh (https://github.com/rubenzantingh/MultiLanguage); only English game text was used, all Danish text is machine-translated.
PatternsX.lua: the English tooltip/message line patterns were taken from PolishForever (data CC BY 4.0, https://github.com/JSlomian/PolishForever); the Danish templates are machine-translated.
